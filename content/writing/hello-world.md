## How posts work

Every post is a Markdown file in `content/writing/`. The filename becomes the
URL, so this one is served at `/writing/hello-world/`. The front matter at the
top sets the title, date, and description:

```markdown
---
title: Your title
date: '2026-09-17'
description: 'One or two sentences. This shows on the index and in link previews.'
---
```

Two optional fields are worth knowing about:

- `image` and `imageAlt` attach a header image, stored under
  `public/images/writing/`.
- `draft: true` keeps a post out of the index, the feed, and the sitemap while
  you work on it.
